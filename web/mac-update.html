Update Foldiq for Mac

A newer version of Foldiq is ready. If you bought Foldiq on foldiq.app, the download link in your order email always gives you the newest version.

  1. Open your Foldiq order email and click Download Foldiq for Mac.
  2. Quit Foldiq, open the new Foldiq.dmg and drag Foldiq into Applications.
  3. Choose Replace. Your settings stay as they were.
Can't find your order email? Contact support

Bought Foldiq on the Mac App Store? It updates through the App Store.
